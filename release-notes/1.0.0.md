- First release: your displays, refresh rates and default audio devices switch automatically when a USB hub connects or disconnects.
- Waits for a TV to wake and for its HDMI audio, and puts the refresh rate back if the TV drops to 60 Hz.
- Switch by hand from the tray, pause automatic switching, and find a hub by unplugging it.
- Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese.
